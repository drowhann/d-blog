# OnlineToolkit: browser tools that keep your data on your machine

I got tired of pasting JSON, passwords, and PDFs into random websites. Most of those jobs do not need a server. The file, the token, the password never had to leave the tab.

That is why I built [OnlineToolkit](https://onlinetoolkit.dev). No account. Most of them run in the browser.

![OnlineToolkit tools directory](/images/onlinetoolkit.png)

## What it is

Five categories: Developer, Text, Security, Calculators, Document. The homepage has search and favorites. Nothing to install. Pick a tool and use it.

The privacy pitch is the product. Format JSON, compress an image, unzip a folder, generate a password. The work stays on your machine. I still would not paste a production secret into any website, including mine. But I also would not send a PDF to a stranger's upload form when the conversion can happen locally.

## Developer

The JSON cluster is what I open most: [formatter](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/json-formatter/), [fixer](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/json-fixer/), and [schema validator](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/json-schema-validator/). Same idea for JWTs and regex: decode or test in the tab, no round trip.

There is also a pile of JSON-to-model generators (TypeScript, Python, Go, Dart, Kotlin, Swift, Zod, C#, Java). Paste a sample, copy a struct. [Local file search](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/local-file-search/) is the one that sounds fake until you try it: point it at a folder, search names and contents, nothing is uploaded.

Color tools sit here too. HEX/RGB/HSL, a palette generator, and a Tailwind v4 shade generator that spits out an `@theme` snippet.

## Text

The boring ones that I actually use: case converter, find and replace (plain or regex), word/character counter, line sorter, slug generator, and a text diff. Lorem ipsum if you are mocking a layout.

## Security

[Password generator](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/strong-password-generator/) and a [strength checker](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/password-strength-checker/). This is the whole point of client-side. If a password tool needs your data on a server, use a different password tool.

## Document

[PDF to image](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/pdf-to-image-converter/), [image compressor](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/image-compressor/), [zip/unzip](https://onlinetoolkit.dev/tools/file-compressor/), video frame extractor, Markdown to PDF. Files stay in the tab. That is the difference between "a converter" and "a converter I will actually drop a receipt or a screenshot into."

## Calculators

SIP and EMI, plus a percentage calculator. Useful, not the reason the site exists.

## Go use it

[onlinetoolkit.dev](https://onlinetoolkit.dev). Free, no signup. I am still adding tools.
